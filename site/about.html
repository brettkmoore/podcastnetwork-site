About

An artist-owned network.

Podcast Network is an independent collective of artist-owned shows.

You own 100% of your show. You keep 90% of the revenue. The network keeps 10%.

The network shares production, ad sales, cross-promotion, and distribution.

Shows stay independent. Listeners get the shows free.

Other terms are discussed with each show.
