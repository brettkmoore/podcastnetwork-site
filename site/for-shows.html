Questions

What is the revenue split?

You own 100% of your show. You keep 90% of the revenue. The network keeps 10%.

What does a show get?

Shared production, ad sales, cross-promotion, and distribution.

What do listeners get?

Free shows. Listening costs nothing.

Who is it for?

Independent, artist-owned shows that want shared support.

Who owns the intellectual property?

You own 100% of the intellectual property of your show.

How long is a contract, and is it exclusive?

Contract length and exclusivity are not set here. Other terms are discussed with each show.

How do I pitch?

Use the form on this page. Name the show, say what it is, and say what you need.
